## Map and original graphics

Ulanqab locator: original map using public-domain Natural Earth geometry. City reference points and straight-line distance calculations are documented in [map credits](../../images/maps/CREDITS.md). Film adaptation crops the map to its geographic area, enlarges city labels and gives the China inset a white panel and stronger regional highlight. There is no verified Galaxy campus pin.

Other diagrams and charts: original explanatory graphics for gian.cool. The 100 kW DC example holds power and resistance constant. Wind/load curves and the battery animation are illustrative, not site measurements. Storage connects as a branch of the conceptual supply path.

## Company marks

The Envision company mark is extracted unchanged from the official site's public

standalone Stargate project logo was verified. Exact files, provenance and rights
