## Map and original graphics

Ulanqab locator: original map using public-domain Natural Earth geometry. City reference points and straight-line distance calculations are documented in [map credits](../../images/maps/CREDITS.md). Film adaptation crops the map to its geographic area and enlarges city labels. There is no verified Galaxy campus pin.

Other diagrams and charts: original explanatory graphics for gian.cool. The 100 kW DC example holds power and resistance constant. Wind/load curves and the battery animation are illustrative, not site measurements. Storage connects as a branch of the conceptual supply path.
