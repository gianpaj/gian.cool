## Title

Why a Wind-Turbine Maker Is Building China's "Stargate"

## Description

Envision makes wind turbines and batteries. Its Galaxy AI campus in Inner Mongolia brings those businesses together with computing.

Following electricity from generation to the rack, we explain founder Lei Zhang's strategy, what an 800-megawatt campus plan means in everyday terms, and why higher-voltage power distribution matters for AI infrastructure.

Original animations show power conversion, changing voltage and current, and batteries balancing an illustrative wind supply. SemiAnalysis compares Galaxy with OpenAI's US Stargate project.

The reporting distinguishes a 120 MW phase reported delivered in August 2026 from the planned 800 MW of IT capacity. Actual server load, installed chips and measured site efficiency remain open questions.
