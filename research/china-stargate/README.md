## Article checked

[We Visited China's Stargate in Inner Mongolia](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/we-visited-chinas-stargate-in-inner), by Everlyn Tan, Jeremie Eliahou Ontiveros, Xiaopeng Laoban and Dylan Patel, published 2026-11-07. Visible byline/date and structured metadata agree. The metadata timestamp is 19:01:39 UTC.

The canonical newsletter page's public preview was read through an ordinary unauthenticated fetch after the web tool failed. The detailed construction, cost, electrical and cooling sections are paywalled and were not read. Only bibliographic metadata and original research notes are saved here, not the full copyrighted article.

## What remains unavailable

- No open-license Galaxy facility photograph was found. The three copyrighted references need permission before reproduction. Two press photos are only 864 × 486 and 1268 × 587 pixels; obtain larger originals for full-screen use. The article figure is 2400 × 1672 pixels and mixes photos with a rendering; do not treat every panel as documentary photography.
- The 800 MW campus plan in SemiAnalysis conflicts in scope with Envision's above-2 GW August and 3 GW November announcements. Current IT load and installed chips remain unverified. Epoch AI's 118 MW is an estimate.
- Cooling equipment classification conflicts across sources. Detailed schematics, measured PUE, water use, actual renewable share and battery MW/MWh ratings were not obtained.
- The article's one-fifth-US-cost comparison cannot be audited from the accessed material. Keep it out of an unqualified headline or narration.

The seven licensed alternatives are usable with their attribution and adaptation
terms. They show regional context, illustrative hardware and the founder; none
establishes Galaxy's installed hardware. Most saved versions are 1920 pixels
wide; the portrait is 1280 × 1857 pixels. All decode successfully and were visually
inspected. The film carries its pinned animation library and local fonts. The
blog app and fax project are unchanged.
