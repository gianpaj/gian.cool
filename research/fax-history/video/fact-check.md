## Numbers that vary by source

- **US fax counts** mix installed base and annual sales. 1987 sales: 295,000 (CAP), 424,600 (NOPA) or 500,000 (Dataquest). Installed base: 4 million by 1989 (EBSCO) vs 5 million by 1990 (ETHW).
- **Finch home receiver**: $79.50 ($49.95 kit), 11 min/page (Invention & Technology) vs $125, ~20 min/page (Radio World). Possibly different models or years.
- **NHS fax machines, 2018**: 11,620 (Labour, WP), 8,946 (Royal College of Surgeons FOI), "more than 8,000" (government). Deadline "31 March 2020" (press release) vs "April 2020" (its summary).
- **Magnafax weight**: 46 lb (WP, BizTech) vs 37 lb (one unverified secondary source).
- **LDX rental prices** ($550 scanner / $650 printer / $800 both) come from a search snippet of an archived Xerox blog. The page now redirects.

## Numbers derived here (recompute if inputs change)

- Raw Group 3 page: 1,728 × 1,145 = 1,978,560 bits.
- Transmission times in `../technology/02-analog-vs-digital.md` are raw bits ÷ rate ÷ compression ratio, ignoring handshake, EOL and minimum-line-time padding.
- Shannon limit: B = 3,100 Hz, S/N = 35 dB → about 36 kbit/s.
- Pantelegraph example price: a 111 × 27 mm slip ≈ 30 cm² × 20 centimes ≈ 6 francs. This assumes the charge applied to the whole sheet area.
