## Books and primary documents

- Jonathan Coopersmith, *Faxed: The Rise and Fall of the Fax Machine* (Johns Hopkins University Press, 2015). The standard history; see `web-research.md`.
- Anton A. Huurdeman, *The Worldwide History of Telecommunications* (Wiley-IEEE, 2003).
- Julia Zons, *Casellis Pantelegraph: Geschichte eines vergessenen Mediums* (PhD, Universität Konstanz, 2015).
- Marcus J. Martin, *The Electrical Transmission of Photographs* (1921). Public domain; Internet Archive IDs `electricaltransm00martrich`, `cu31924031219680`.
- "Transmitting Photographs by Telegraph", *Scientific American*, 12 May 1877, p. 297.
- Shelford Bidwell, "Tele-Photography", *Nature*, 10 Feb 1881; "Telegraphic Photography and Electric Vision", *Nature*, 4 June 1908.
- Harry Nyquist, "Certain Factors Affecting Telegraph Speed" (1924); "Certain Topics in Telegraph Transmission Theory" (1928).
- Claude Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" (1948).
- David A. Huffman, "A Method for the Construction of Minimum-Redundancy Codes" (1952).
- Alexander Bain, British patent 9745 (27 May 1843). Giovanni Caselli, US patent 37,563 (1863). Elisha Gray, telautograph patent (31 July 1888).
- ITU-T Recommendations T.4, T.6, T.30, T.38, T.82/T.85 and V.21/V.27ter/V.29/V.17/V.34. Free PDFs at https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-T/en
