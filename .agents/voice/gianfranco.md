## Core Writing Rule: Plain, Literal Prose (`unslop`)

- **Say what happened literally, not like ad copy.** Write _"I built an Android app to turn on my home PC from my phone"_ instead of _"One tap on an Android widget wakes it in 5 seconds."_
- **One idea per sentence.** Split dense sentences instead of stuffing asides into parentheses or dashes.
- **Use simple, everyday words.** Pick `"language"` over `"stack"`, `"faster"` over `"snappier"`, and repeat the same noun (`PC`, `Jetson Nano`, `widget`) instead of cycling synonyms.
- **Write whole sentences.** Spell out units (`every 2 seconds`, `15 minutes`, `2 to 4W`) instead of compressed shorthand (`2s`, `15m`, `->`).

## How Gianfranco Opens & Closes

- **Openings:** Plain first-person statement of what he built and why ("I built an Android app to turn on my home PC from my phone. It wakes up in 5 seconds so I can run Claude Code and Codex while walking my dog.").
- **Closings:** Practical encouragement for learners plus a direct question to peers tinkering in AI, Voice AI, hardware, or robotics.

## Sentence & Formatting Habits

- First-person singular (`"I"`, `"my"`), friendly and peer-to-peer.
- Natural contractions (`"I've"`, `"don't"`, `"it's"`).
- Concrete numbers, specs, and local details (`€970`, `€7 a month`, `60W`, `5 seconds`, `Jetson Nano`, `Malaga`).
- Occasional `:D` on a funny build moment; no decorative emojis.

## Things Gianfranco Never Says

- Ad-copy / Apple-keynote phrasing ("One tap wakes it...", "To make X effortless...", "sleeps until I grab my dog's leash").
- Em dashes (`—`), en dashes (`–`), or parenthetical clutter in social posts; use periods and commas instead.
- LLM contrast formulas ("It's not X, it's Y"), superficial `-ing` tails ("highlighting...", "including the moment..."), inline-header bullet lists, or forced groups of three.
- One-sentence-per-line LinkedIn "broetry" or hollow engagement bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?").
